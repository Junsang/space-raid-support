Support

For gameplay, saves, accessibility, advertising or privacy questions, email dev@junsangs.com.

Include device/OS/App version, affected screen, reproduction steps and expected/actual results, with a screenshot when possible. Do not send passwords, payment details or another person's information.

An interstitial may appear when leaving completed-run Scores and grants no game reward. When required, reopen Home > Privacy > Ad privacy choices to change choices. Non-personalized ads still process data.

Age choices

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13+. A local age band controls advertising eligibility; no birth date or exact age is requested. Unknown/Not now/Under 13 does not call either SDK initialization or request ads. When eligible, the SDKs receive the underage-treatment flag. Change the band in Privacy; prepared ads are discarded and changes apply next launch.

13세 이상 일반 이용자용 앱으로 생년월일·정확한 나이 대신 기기 내 구간만 선택합니다. 미선택·나중에 선택·13세 미만은 앱이 두 SDK 초기화·광고 요청을 호출하지 않습니다. Privacy에서 변경하면 광고를 폐기하고 다음 실행부터 새 설정을 적용합니다.

Offline play and saves

Core gameplay works offline without an account. Progress stays local; publisher cloud restoration is not available. Clearing App data or deleting the App can permanently remove local progress. OS backup/migration follows device/account settings.

Google ads/privacy messages may need a connection. Ad unavailability must not trap game navigation. Google advertising data is separate from local saves and follows Google's policies and available controls.
